## Description (What it does.)
End-to-end social media management for brands that need strategy,
production, and scheduling in one place. Reads your brand config
(voice, pillars, platforms, banned words), ideates against your
content pillars, produces assets via AI image and video tools,
writes native captions per platform, runs a pre-publish gate, and
schedules into peak windows.

Handles the full loop: intake → strategy → ideate → produce → write
→ assemble → schedule → publish → analyse → iterate. Maintains a
running `learnings.md` to track what hooks, formats, and times work
best for your account. Repurposes one asset across multiple
platforms and formats (one 9:16 master video feeds Instagram Reels,
TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook Stories).

Integrates with Ayrshare, Postiz, n8n, native platform APIs, and
Telegram for approval workflows. Generates copy, carousel slides,
short-form video scripts, and talking-head content briefs. Measures
against the goal that matters — reach for awareness, saves/shares
for engagement, link clicks for leads, conversions for sales.

## What's inside.
01. Brand config template (voice, pillars, platforms, banned words,
