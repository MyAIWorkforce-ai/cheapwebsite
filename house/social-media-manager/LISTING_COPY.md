## Description (What it does.)
End-to-end social media management for brands and agencies that need
strategy, production, and scheduling in one place. Reads your brand
config (voice, pillars, platforms, banned words, region), ideates
against your content pillars, produces assets via AI image and video
tools, writes native captions per platform, runs a hard pre-publish
gate, schedules into peak windows, and triages every reply.

Handles the full loop: intake → strategy → calendar → ideate →
production briefs → captions + scripts → hashtag stacks →
pre-publish gate → schedule + Telegram approval → engagement triage
→ weekly report → learnings update. Maintains a running `learnings.md`
to track what hooks, formats, hashtag stacks, and times work best for
your account.

Repurposes one asset across multiple platforms and formats (one 9:16
master video feeds Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook
Reels, and LinkedIn vertical-video without re-shooting).

Integrates with Buffer, Hootsuite, Later, Sprout Social, Sked Social,
Pallyy, Publer, SocialBee, Ayrshare, Postiz, n8n, Meta Business Suite,
TikTok Business Suite, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, and YouTube Studio.
Telegram approval flow for human sign-off before anything goes live.

Generates copy, carousel slides, short-form and long-form video
scripts, talking-head briefs, hashtag stacks, influencer outreach
DMs, monthly client reports (for agencies), and a crisis-comms script
for the day a post catches fire.

Measures against the goal that matters — reach for awareness,
saves/shares for engagement, link clicks for leads, conversions for
sales.

Works in five regions out of the box — Australia, New Zealand, the UK,
the US, and Canada — with disclosure phrasing (AANA / ASA / FTC /
Competition Bureau / ASA NZ), regulators (ACCC, CMA, FCA, FDA, SEC,
OQLF), spelling conventions, and calendar (Anzac Day, Remembrance Day,
Truth & Reconciliation Day, Independence Day, Saint-Jean-Baptiste,
Black Friday, EOFY) all mapped.
