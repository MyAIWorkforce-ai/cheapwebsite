## Description (What it does.)

A full builder's office for your agent. Drop this bundle in, tell
it your builder licence, insurance, preferred contract (HIA / MBA /
NZS 3915 / JCT / AIA / CCDC), base rates, deposit cap rules,
retention policy, subbie roster, and supplier accounts — and it
runs the desk.

Handles the full project lifecycle: enquiry arrives ("thinking
about a rear extension, ballpark?") → qualify → site visit + concept
→ fixed-price or cost-plus quote with PC schedule, allowances, and
the variation mechanism baked in → contract → deposit invoice (cap-
aware) → council approval pathway (DA + CC + OC in AU; Building
Consent + CCC in NZ; Planning + Building Regs in UK; Permit +
Inspections in US/CA) → subbie scheduling + materials coordination
→ stage-by-stage progress claims (deposit → slab → frame → lock-up
→ fix-out → PC → retention) → variations with written sign-off →
handover pack (Occupation Cert / CCC / CofO + defects schedule +
warranties index) → defects liability period management → 11-month
sweep (the single highest-ROI service most builders skip) →
retention release at 12 months → quarterly relationship touch.
Then repeats. Forever.

Universal across Australia, New Zealand, the UK, the US, and
Canada — the regional reference inside the bundle maps every term
(HIA + MBA vs NZS 3915 vs JCT vs AIA vs CCDC; DA + CC + OC vs
Building Consent + CCC vs Planning + Building Regs vs Permit +
Inspections; state-specific Home Warranty insurance thresholds; PC
items + variations; lock-up vs weather-tight; fit-off vs 2nd-fix)
so you don't need to teach the agent which country you're in.

Builder-specific built in: knows the difference between a small
job / handyman / maintenance enquiry (under $5k, callout-style) and
a project (where the site visit + concept + budget conversation has
to happen before the quote). Knows that fixed-price and cost-plus
are fundamentally different beasts and the contract language has
to match. Knows that "the slab", "the frame", "going to lockup",
"fit-off", "PC", "variations", "PCs" (prime cost items), "the
certifier", "the inspector", "the subbie", "rough-in", and
"defects liability period" are different terms with different
implications. Knows that the verbal variation is the #1 way
builders lose money and forces written sign-off before extra work.
Knows that the 11-month sweep before the 12-month defects period
ends is the highest-ROI service most builders never offer (90%+
referral conversion when you DO offer it).

Maintains a running `learnings.md` so the agent gets sharper every
project — tracks which job types win on margin (kitchen reno vs
extension vs new build vs commercial fit-out), which subbies show
up on the booked day, which suppliers actually deliver to site on
time, which PC items clients chronically overspend on, where
variations slip without sign-off, and which architects send
quality leads vs window-shoppers. Update the rates once; every
later quote, contract, and progress claim respects them.

Built for residential builders, small commercial builders, general
contractors, design-and-construct outfits, and renovation
specialists who want to spend more time on tools and on site, and
less time chasing paperwork.
