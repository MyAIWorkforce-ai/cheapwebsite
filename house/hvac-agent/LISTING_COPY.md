## Description (What it does.)

A full HVAC business desk for your agent. Drop this bundle in, tell
it your hourly rate, service area, refrigerant licence type and tier
(ARC RHL / EPA 608 / F-Gas / TSSA), and it runs the whole desk:
intake, quoting, dispatch, refrigerant logbook + handover packs,
invoicing, follow-ups, supplier orders, after-hours triage, annual
service plans, and weekly reports.

Handles the full loop: text arrives ("AC not cooling, 38° outside,
baby in the house") → qualify the lead → quote within 15 minutes →
schedule → on-the-way SMS → on-site → refrigerant log + handover
pack → invoice with payment link → next-day check-in → 3-day review
request → 7-day service plan ask → end-of-week report + learnings
update. Then repeats. Forever.

Universal across Australia, New Zealand, the UK, the US, and Canada —
the regional reference inside the bundle maps every term (ARC RHL vs
EPA 608 vs F-Gas REFCOM vs ODSHAR, GST vs VAT, R32 vs R410A vs R454B,
AS/NZS 5149 vs BS EN 378 vs CSA B52) so you don't need to teach the
agent which country you're in.

HVAC-specific built in: knows the difference between a callout (no
cool, no heat, drip, noisy) and a project (split changeout, ducted
install, heat pump retrofit, RTU swap). Knows that refrigerant
handling is a licensed activity — won't quote a recover/recharge job
without a current licence on file. Knows that a system low on
refrigerant needs a leak find + repair, NOT a top-up (illegal in most
regions, brand damage everywhere). Knows that the indoor unit is
"the head" on a wall split, "the cassette" on a ceiling install,
"the fan coil" on a ducted. Knows that the first heatwave of summer
and the first frost of winter are the highest-margin weeks of the
year.

Builds the annual service plan into every customer touchpoint —
because that's the recurring-revenue spine of an HVAC business. The
service plan template + skill is the strongest in the bundle (60-70%
attach target on installs, 30-40% on callouts, contracts can double
customer lifetime value).

Maintains a running `learnings.md` so the agent gets sharper every
week — tracks which job types win on margin (split changeouts vs
diagnostic callouts), which suburbs convert on service plans, what
your heatwave-week capacity looks like, which suppliers stock-out on
R32 cylinders going into summer. Update the rates once; every later
quote respects them.

Built for solo HVAC techs and small refrigeration/AC firms who want
to spend more time on the tools and less in front of a screen.
