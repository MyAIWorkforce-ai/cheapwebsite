## Description (What it does.)

A full electrician business desk for your agent. Drop this bundle in,
tell it your hourly rate, service area, and licence number, and it
runs the whole desk: intake, quoting, dispatch, compliance
certificates, invoicing, follow-ups, supplier orders, after-hours
triage, and weekly reports.

Handles the full loop: text arrives → qualify the lead → quote within
15 minutes → schedule → on-the-way SMS → on-site → compliance cert →
invoice with payment link → next-day check-in → 3-day review request
→ end-of-week report + learnings update. Then repeats. Forever.

Universal across Australia, New Zealand, the UK, the US, and Canada —
the regional reference inside the bundle maps every term (COC vs EICR
vs Permit/Inspection, GST vs VAT, AS/NZS 3000 vs BS 7671 vs NEC vs
CEC) so you don't need to teach the agent which country you're in.

Maintains a running `learnings.md` so the agent gets sharper every
week — tracks which job types win on margin, which suburbs have
shortest drive times, which customer types convert at quote stage,
and what your no-show rate looks like. Update the rates once; every
later quote respects them.

Built for solo sparkies and small electrical firms who want to spend
more time on the tools and less in front of a screen.
