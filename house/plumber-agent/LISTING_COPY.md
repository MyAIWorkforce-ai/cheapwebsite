## Description (What it does.)

A full plumbing business desk for your agent. Drop this bundle in,
tell it your hourly rate, service area, plumbing licence and gas
ticket (if you do gas), and it runs the whole desk: intake, quoting,
dispatch, compliance certificates, invoicing, follow-ups, supplier
orders, after-hours triage, and weekly reports.

Handles the full loop: text arrives ("burst pipe under the sink") →
qualify the lead → quote within 15 minutes → schedule → on-the-way
SMS → on-site → compliance cert → invoice with payment link →
next-day check-in → 3-day review request → end-of-week report +
learnings update. Then repeats. Forever.

Universal across Australia, New Zealand, the UK, the US, and Canada —
the regional reference inside the bundle maps every term (Compliance
Certificate vs WRAS vs Permit/Inspection, gas Type A vs Gas Safe vs
TSSA, GST vs VAT, AS/NZS 3500 vs UPC/IPC) so you don't need to teach
the agent which country you're in.

Plumbing-specific built in: knows the difference between a callout
(leaking tap, blocked toilet) and a project (hot water replacement,
bathroom reno, gas line install). Knows that gas fitting is a
separate ticket and won't quote it without one. Knows that
"the head" is the toilet, "the cistern" is the tank, and "the trap"
is the U-bend. Knows that burst pipes at 11pm in July are the
highest-margin emergency work you'll ever do.

Maintains a running `learnings.md` so the agent gets sharper every
week — tracks which job types win on margin (hot water swap vs
blocked drain), which suburbs have the shortest drive times, which
customer types convert at quote stage, and what your after-hours
conversion looks like. Update the rates once; every later quote
respects them.

Built for solo plumbers and small plumbing firms who want to spend
more time on the tools and less in front of a screen.
