# Skillzy — 75-second website explainer

> **One master video.** Built around screen-recordings of skillzy.ai
> itself. Each beat tells you exactly which page to record, what to
> click, and what the voiceover says over the top.

**Total run:** ~75 seconds (~12-15 seconds slack for transitions).
**Aspect:** 9:16 vertical for Reels / TikTok / Shorts. Crop a 16:9
desktop recording to the centre column for vertical export.
**Music:** light editorial piano or quiet ambient — never EDM, never
upbeat marketing music. Skillzy is a quiet brand.
**On-screen typeface:** Fraunces serif for any overlays, gold
`#C19E50` on the cream backdrop the site already provides.

---

## How to record the source clips

Tools that work:

- **macOS QuickTime** → File → New Screen Recording → Selection → drag
  over Safari window. Free, native, perfect quality.
- **Chrome/Safari mobile** → iOS Screen Recording (Control Centre).
  Use this if you're recording mobile views.
- **Loom** if you want cursor highlights baked in.
- **ScreenStudio** (Mac, paid) if you want auto-zoom on clicks.

Record each beat as a **separate clip** so you can re-take any single
shot without redoing the whole video. Aim for ~2 sec longer than the
beat length below — the editor trims.

## The script — beat by beat

### Beat 1 — Hero (0:00 → 0:06)

**Record:** desktop skillzy.ai homepage, full viewport. Stay still for
2 sec, then slow scroll down ~200px to reveal the "Drop in. Your
agent just got smarter." headline. Stop scrolling.

**On-screen overlay (3-6s):** small Fraunces caption fades up bottom-
left: *"a marketplace for agent skills"*

**Voiceover:**
> "Skillzy. Marketplace for the skills, guides, and full agent setups that already work."

---

### Beat 2 — The Marketplace grid (0:06 → 0:14)

**Record:** click "Marketplace" from the nav. Land on `/marketplace`.
Wait for the dropdown filters + product grid to render. Slow scroll
through 3-4 product cards.

**On-screen overlay (10-13s):** small caption right side: *"Built by
creators in the field"*

**Voiceover:**
> "Real Estate agents. Bookkeepers. Tradies. The setups they built for themselves — now you can drop them in."

---

### Beat 3 — Product detail (0:14 → 0:22)

**Record:** click into a product — pick the **Real Estate, end to end**
listing. Show the page rendering: hero, tagline, "What you get"
bullets, price, "Buy" button. Pause on the price.

**On-screen overlay (18-21s):** "From $79." big gold caption appears
next to the price.

**Voiceover:**
> "Each one is a working bundle. SKILL.md, system prompts, setup guide, configs. Drop it in. It runs."

---

### Beat 4 — The Sell page (0:22 → 0:30)

**Record:** navigate to `/sell`. Show the **"Five steps. Minutes,
not days."** headline. Slow scroll past the 5 numbered tiles
(Sign up, List it, Let it describe itself, Connect Stripe, Get paid).

**On-screen overlay:** none — let the site's own steps grid do the talking.

**Voiceover:**
> "And if you've built a skill yourself — selling it takes about five minutes."

---

### Beat 5 — Drop the file (0:30 → 0:42) — **THE MAGIC MOMENT**

**Record:** click "Start your listing" → land on `/sell/new`. Scroll
past the **"What is it?"** type tiles (pick **Skill** so it shows the
gold ring around the selected tile). Pause on the "Drop your file in"
dropzone.

Then drop a real `.md` or `.pdf` file into the dropzone (use one of
your actual existing skill files — `SKILL.md` from a repo). Record
the field-by-field auto-fill as the AI drafts: Title appears, then
Tagline, then "What it does" bullets, then "What buyers get". This is
the single best 12 seconds of the whole video.

**On-screen overlay (38-42s):** small caption fades up:
*"Drafted by AI. Edit anything."*

**Voiceover:**
> "Drop your file. Skillzy reads it. Writes the title, the blurb, the bullets — in your voice. You change anything you don't like."

---

### Beat 6 — Set price + Publish (0:42 → 0:52)

**Record:** scroll down to the **"Price"** field. Type `$19`. Scroll
past "Niche" and "Works with" multi-select chips — open the Niche
popup briefly to show the tick chips, close it again. Land on the
**Publish** button. Click it.

**On-screen overlay (48-52s):** "live in 30s" caption animates up
beside the button.

**Voiceover:**
> "Pick a price. Tick the niches it fits. Hit publish."

---

### Beat 7 — Listing live (0:52 → 0:60)

**Record:** auto-redirect to `/sell/new/done` (or the live listing
page). Hold on the new listing's URL bar — `skillzy.ai/marketplace/your-slug` —
for a beat so viewers see it's a real public page.

**On-screen overlay:** "Live." in gold Fraunces, centred bottom.

**Voiceover:**
> "Live. Buyers can pay you right now."

---

### Beat 8 — The good email (0:60 → 0:68)

**Record:** switch to your inbox (Gmail, Apple Mail, anything). Show
the sale notification email arriving — *"You just sold 'X' on Skillzy."*
Pan over the email body so the "Payout: $15.20" line is visible.

**On-screen overlay (64-68s):** mono caption animates next to the
payout figure: *"80% to your bank. Stripe pays direct."*

**Voiceover:**
> "Every sale lands in your inbox. Eighty percent of it lands in your bank, straight from Stripe."

---

### Beat 9 — Dashboard (0:68 → 0:73)

**Record:** open `/dashboard?view=selling`. Show the **Earnings /
Sales / Listings / Next payout** stats grid at the top, with real
numbers if you have them (or sensible demo numbers).

**On-screen overlay:** none — the dashboard speaks for itself.

**Voiceover:**
> "Every listing. Every sale. One dashboard."

---

### Beat 10 — Close (0:73 → 0:75)

**Record:** cut to a clean cream background (you can use the homepage
hero scrolled back to top, or a still card you drop in). The Skillzy
wordmark appears in gold Fraunces, big and centred. Tagline italic
gold underneath: *"Your agent just got smarter."* URL fades in mono:
SKILLZY.AI

**Voiceover:**
> "Skillzy.ai. Drop one in."

## Edit notes

- **Cuts:** straight cuts everywhere. No swooshes, no slides, no
  whip-pans. Skillzy is editorial, not promo.
- **Cursor:** keep cursor visible but small. ScreenStudio or
  Loom can add a soft circle so it doesn't disappear.
- **Speed:** all clips can be sped up 1.2–1.5× to make the flow feel
  snappy without rushing. The Beat 5 file-drop → AI-draft beat should
  stay at real-time speed — the magic IS the live drafting.
- **Captions:** burn them in (don't rely on the platform to add
  them). 25% of viewers will watch without sound — every overlay
  above is a redundant capture of the voiceover line.
- **Outro card:** keep it for 2 full seconds. Resist the urge to
  cram more in. The blank cream + gold wordmark is the entire brand.

## Three variant edits to cut from the same master

Once you've recorded the 10 beats, you can produce:

1. **75-second hero version** (above) — Reels, TikTok, embed on the
   homepage as a `<video>` autoplay.
2. **30-second teaser** — keep Beats 1, 5, 7, 10. Voiceover trimmed
   to "Skillzy. Drop your file. Listing live in 30 seconds. Skillzy.ai."
3. **15-second loop** — just Beat 5 on repeat (the file-drop and
   auto-draft moment). Silent. Use as an Instagram Story or a header
   hover-loop on /sell.
