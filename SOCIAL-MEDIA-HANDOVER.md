## 5. Voice & tone

**Short sentences. No filler. Confident, not corporate.**

Copy the site's rhythm:

- *"Drop in. Agent supercharged."*
- *"The whole agent. Best margins."*
- *"Handles the whole pipeline. Writes the follow-ups. Never drops a
  lead."*
- *"Nothing matched. Try less."*

**Rules:**

- Say "drop in" a lot. That's the verb.
- Say "your agent just got smarter" or variations — that's the win.
- Never use "solution," "leverage," "empower," "unlock," "harness,"
  "seamless," "revolutionise."
- Never write in headline-title-case for body copy.
- Curly quotes and em-dashes are fine. Emoji sparingly (a ✿ or a → is
  on-brand; a rocket emoji is not).
- If in doubt: read a sentence out loud. If it sounds like a keynote,
  cut it.

**Names:**

- Product is **Skillzy** (capital S, everything after is lowercase).
- Domain is **skillzy.ai** (never .com — we don't own it).
- The types are: **Skills, Guides, Prompt Packs, MCP Servers, Loops,
  Agent Setups.** Capital S on the type name in copy.

row.

For people who don't know what a Skill or MCP server is.

### D. Category deep-dives
Pick a trade or role, show what's on the marketplace for it.
- Real estate agents
- Plumbers / trades

- Freelance designers
- Solo founders

### E. Platform news + integrations
When Claude/Cursor/Windsurf ship something new, tie it back.
- New Anthropic model → "Every Skill on Skillzy works with the new
  Claude."

## 8. Do / Don't

**DO**
- Show real listings, real prices, real creators (with permission).
- Reply to comments in Skillzy's voice — short, useful, human.
- Link to the specific listing or category, not just the homepage.
- Repost creators' own launch content (with credit).
- Use vertical video for TikTok / Reels / Shorts. Under 45s.

**DON'T**
- Don't claim "Melbourne-based." We are a **global marketplace.** No
  city.
- Don't name the founder or the operating entity publicly. It's just
  **Skillzy.ai**. (Ops details live in /privacy /terms — don't repeat
  them on social.)
- Don't promise "no fees" — creators pay 20% platform fee. Say
  **creators keep 80%.**
- Don't say the marketplace is "coming soon." It's live. Listings are
  buyable today.
- Don't compare Skillzy directly to specific competitors by name
  unless the caption is airtight. Stay positive.
- Don't post about the internal Wise/Stripe/payout plumbing. Buyers
  don't care. Creators only need to know they connect once and get
  paid.

## 10. Common mix-ups to avoid

- **Grok Boys / Companions** (Ani, Rudi, Valentine) — these are xAI's
  in-app characters. They're **not** something you can buy on
  Skillzy. Skillzy sells skills/prompts/agents that **target** Grok
  as a platform, not the companions themselves.
- **Claude Skills** — this is Anthropic's own name for a distribution
  format (SKILL.md files). We didn't invent the name; we're the
  marketplace for them. Always capitalised.
- **MCP** = **Model Context Protocol.** Open standard from Anthropic.
  MCP servers give agents tools + data. Don't spell it out unless the
  audience is technical.
- **Skillzy is not a chatbot.** It doesn't answer questions. It sells
  the files/configs that make other agents smarter.
